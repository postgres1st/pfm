# Code of Conduct

This applies to everyone taking part in the PFMM project — issues, pull requests, code
review, discussions and any other project space.

## What we expect

Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagreement is normal and useful; contempt is not.
Critique the work, not the person. Accept that maintainers have to weigh trade-offs you
may not see, and that a change can be declined without it being a judgement on you.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberately derailing them

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to
<contact@postgresfirst.com>. Reports are treated confidentially, and we will respond as
quickly as we can.

Include what happened, where, and — if you can — links or other specifics. You do not need
to be the target of the behaviour to report it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for applying this code, and may edit or remove contributions,
comments, issues and pull requests that conflict with it. Serious or repeated breaches may
result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

Enforcement decisions are made by the maintainers. If you believe a decision was taken in
error, say so at the address above.
